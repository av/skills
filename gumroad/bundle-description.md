### Why skills?

AI agents are only as good as their instructions. A vague prompt gets vague results. A SKILL.md gives your agent:

- **Decision trees** — not "try something" but "if X, do Y; if Z, do W"
- **Pitfall documentation** — hard-won lessons so your agent doesn't repeat mistakes
- **Verification checklists** — proof that the work is actually done
- **Real code patterns** — copy-paste-ready, not theoretical examples
